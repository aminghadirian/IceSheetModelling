Floating Array — Equivalent-Plate Screening

A large moored floating array (many small floaters) modelled as an orthotropic thin plate on water. Flexural-gravity (hydroelastic) screening for global response and connector loads. Screening only — not a design-load tool.

Screening model: linear orthotropic-plate flexural-gravity hydroelasticity. Structure and hydrodynamics are homogenised together (no per-floater RAO/QTF). Effective Dₓ, D_y, η and areal mass must be calibrated from a unit-cell sub-model; extreme/nonlinear connector and mooring loads require local time-domain modelling. See the README for assumptions and limits.
