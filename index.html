Screening model: single-frequency variable-coefficient mild-slope wave equation, time-marched to steady state with sponge boundaries. The platform is a region of modified flexural-gravity wavenumber (no per-floater RAO/QTF; not full hydroelastic edge mechanics). Calibrate Dₓ, D_y, η, areal mass from a unit-cell sub-model and verify extreme loads with local time-domain modelling. See the README.
