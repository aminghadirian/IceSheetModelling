Wave Scattering by a Floating Elastic Plate

1D hydroelastic scattering of a water wave by a finite floating elastic plate, solved by eigenfunction matching with free-edge conditions. Reflection / transmission, deflection and bending loads — with a built-in energy-conservation check.

Wave

Floating plate

Linear hydroelastic theory (Fox & Squire-type eigenfunction matching). Energy conservation R²+T²=1 (no damping) is computed every run as a correctness check. The plate is a homogenised continuum: effective D, areal mass and loss factor must be calibrated from a unit-cell sub-model; extreme/nonlinear connector and mooring loads need local time-domain modelling.
